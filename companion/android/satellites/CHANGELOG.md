## 0.180.0

- **Time slots for a sensor, and no more duplicate sensors.** A sensor's action can have different looks at different times of day (day bright, evening a scene, night dim): add time slots in the sensor's editor, the first one that covers the time decides, and any other time uses the sensor's own look, which can now set a brightness. A Hue sensor's own switches (Motion sensor enabled, Light sensor enabled) show in its settings. The Sensor actions list no longer includes Ring cameras' motion or Hue rooms, only the sensors themselves.

## 0.179.0

- **A light's settings page follows the approved design.** A room's lights are listed in one card ("LIGHTS"), each with its type as well as its level and a chevron. A room with a ring of segments, like the Cloakroom, shows the dial: the main light in the middle and a piece of the ring per segment, each in its colour; tap the middle or a piece to edit it. The ring (Up light) opens as one light with the colour controls. In the hybrid scene editor, Gradient is only offered to lights that can do one, and Dynamic only to Hue lights.

## 0.178.0

- **Sensor actions, and the Lights page's Automations section.** The Routines section on the Lights page is now **Automations**, and under Light routines it has a Sensor actions card: one row per motion or presence sensor (the Cloakroom's, and the Hue ones), showing what it does and who runs it. Open one to choose what its lights do when someone's there and when the room empties, with "only when it's dark" and "between set times", and to set the sensor's own hold time and sensitivity. A Hue sensor keeps its lights on the Hue bridge until you set it up here, which asks you to switch its Hue automation off first.

## 0.177.0

- **A room's settings show its scenes.** Press and hold a room (the Cloakroom, say) and its settings now list the room's own scenes, the same list as on its card, above its hybrid scenes. The long effects list on the Govee light is gone from a light's settings.

## 0.176.0

- **Light effects.** A light that has effects (the Govee Cloakroom light's sunrise, candlelight, twinkle and the rest) now shows an Effects row in its settings, with None to stop one. The Cloakroom light is linked to the Cloakroom room and the Ground Floor summary.

## 0.175.1

- **Upcoming heating items open Heating details.** Tapping a heating item in Upcoming now opens the Heating details page (Back returns to Upcoming), instead of doing nothing.
- **Slide-up sheets start above the bottom bar.** The thermostat's Clive settings, the room range and the To-Do assistant now rise from above the app's bottom bar (or the phone's navigation bar), not from the very bottom of the screen. Every app's sheets use the one `BarSheet`.

## 0.170.0

- **Add to the house's sections.** Open a section in Customise this page and choose Add to this section: cards of the same kind from the house's pages, or any single light or device of that kind (more rooms, zones or lights in Lights; other locks, sensors and cameras in Security; and so on), found by name. ## 0.169.0

- **Your own layout.** A Customise this page button on Home, Security, Climate, Cleaning and To-do lets you order the sections, switch them on or off, and (where the cards come from the dashboard) hide and reorder a section's cards. On Home you can make your own sections with cards from any page, with an icon and a colour (Auto, Dynamic or a palette). It is kept in the house against your Home Assistant user, so a new phone looks the same; Reset puts a page, or every page, back as the house has it. Lighting, Devices and Energy keep the house's layout for now.

## 0.168.0

- **Saved colours live in the house.** The colours kept for a light's colour settings (up to eight) are the same on every phone. Clive Admin has a Saved colours page in the account panel to take them out or add one with a colour wheel. Colours already saved on a phone are sent up the first time the house has none.

## 0.167.0

- **Hybrid scenes.** One tap gives each light in a room its own look (leave it, off, a white scene, a colour, a gradient or a dynamic scene). A room's card shows them as split-colour tiles under its scenes; the room's page has a Hybrid scenes section where an administrator makes one, picks its icon from a short list and presses and holds a tile to change it. They are kept in the house, so every phone sees the same ones.

## 0.166.0

- **A new look for a light's settings.** A power bar at the top switches the light; brightness comes first and moves in 5% steps; the white tone, colour and scenes are sections that open when you edit them. Colour lights have presets, saved colours (up to eight, kept on the phone) and an eyedropper button that opens the colour wheel; white lights have the white scenes under the temperature bar. A room, zone or group opens to a list of just its lights.

## 0.165.3

## 0.82.0

- Pairing (Phase 3): the Add tab scans the QR code on a satellite's screen or sticker (or takes its name and six digits by hand), sends it the home Wi-Fi over Bluetooth (Improv), adds it to Home Assistant's ESPHome integration, waits for it to appear and opens it to set up. Remove from Home Assistant and Reset pairing are under Actions. Not yet tried on a real satellite.
- Whole-house management (Phase 2): presets (save a satellite's design, glances, backlight, night hours, volume and switches under a name; apply to any satellites), copying one satellite's settings to others, a Voice section with Home Assistant's own assistant, wake-word and finished-speaking choices, and warnings on the list ("Weak Wi-Fi", "Offline for 30 min"). A satellite with no screen is listed too, without the design, glance and backlight sections. `satellite/ha/clive-satellite-health.yaml` sends a Home Assistant notification when one is offline or weak for a while.
- Day-to-day control (Phase 1): a Status card (Wi-Fi in words and dBm, network, address, firmware, running time, when it went offline), a Sound section (speaker volume and "Say hello"), Name and room (rename, move to a room), Actions (flash the screen, clean screen, restart with a confirmation) and a Firmware card with Install when Home Assistant offers an update. Each shows only what the satellite has, so older firmware still works. Renaming and rooms need a Home Assistant administrator.
- Signs in automatically through the Church Drive app (0.82.0 or later) now that its Play signing certificate is trusted.
