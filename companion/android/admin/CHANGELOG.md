# Clive Admin: changelog

## 0.127.0

- An About page: the version and build, where updates come from, and this app's signing certificate (SHA-256) with a Copy button, so it can be added to the trusted list for the Church Drive app's shared sign-in.
- The phone app now fits phones on their side and tablets, in the shared version.

## 0.126.0

- The first version of Clive Admin, the app for the house's administrators.
- Signs in with the Home Assistant login, or uses the Church Drive app's sign-in if it is on the phone and signed in. Anyone who is not an administrator is told so.
- Colour palettes, view only: every palette as the house has it now, with the ones changed in Home Assistant marked. Palettes are changed on the website.
- Placeholder logo and splash until Clive Admin's own are chosen.
- Manager, Energy and Device Health are still in the Church Drive phone app; they move here one at a time once this app is proven.
