Satellite device icons

Six ideas for the icon that stands for a satellite or smart speaker, drawn in the Church Drive way: one flat shape, the details cut out of it, Clive's rounded square tucked in as an option.

The shapes come from your sheet: the tall cylinder, the low puck, the dome, the speaker with waves, and our own round screen. Each is one solid shape with a light, a dock or a speaker grille cut out of it. "With Clive's block" tucks a small rounded square into the top-right corner and cuts it out where they overlap, as on the house and the clipboard. Each card shows the icon large, as the bottom-bar tab (26 px), as a satellite's row (the circle in the list) and as the "On the satellite" header.
