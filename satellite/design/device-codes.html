Clive device codes

Every satellite gets one permanent code, printed on its sticker and shown in the app. It is the ID you read out or type if the QR code won't scan, what the satellite calls itself over Bluetooth while pairing, and what tells two identical boxes apart in the list. It is not the six digits on a screen: those change each time and only check that you are looking at the right device. The labels are drawn at your 14 × 60 mm size, with a made-up QR code.

What the code is used for. The QR code carries it, so scanning needs no typing. The satellite uses it as its Bluetooth name (clive-7k3q) and its network name, and the app shows it under the satellite's name until you rename it. Each satellite also gets its own secret key at flash time, which is never printed in readable form: it is only inside the QR code.
