Settings cog ideas

Six cogs that belong with Clive and the house, instead of the Material one. Each is one flat shape with the hub cut out, drawn on the same rounded-square grammar as the logos.

The Material cog is at the top for comparison. Each card shows the cog large, at the size of an account-panel row (28 px) and a small one (20 px), and as the badge on the satellite icon, which is where it would be used for "Each satellite's own settings".
