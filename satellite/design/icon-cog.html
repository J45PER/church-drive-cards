Settings cog, rounder

The real Material cog, six spokes, taken from the icon font itself and not redrawn. Only the corners are rounded, and the outer spokes more than the rest.

The first card is the Material cog as it is (the solid and the outlined one). The three after it round it by three amounts: the tips of the six outer spokes get the extra rounding and every other corner, the roots between the spokes and the hub, only a light one. In the outline version the inner line is rounded in step so the line keeps its width. Each card shows the solid and the outline cog large, at row size (28 px) and small (20 px), and the solid one as the badge on the satellite icon.
