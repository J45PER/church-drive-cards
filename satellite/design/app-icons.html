Satellites app icons

Eight ways to give the Satellites app its own Clive look, drawn from the satellite's own screen: the black ground, the three-colour glow, the white mark and its dock.

Every icon is shown as Android masks it (circle and squircle) and at home-screen sizes, with the splash in light and dark. The splash would use the same artwork in the circle above the name, instead of the bare mark the other apps use. Tap "Pick this one" on your favourite, or tell me the letters of two to combine.
