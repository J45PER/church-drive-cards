Clive Admin: the shield, with different middles

The shield is the house's own (the Church Drive shield from the icon set, not a new drawing). Clive's two squares turn into it: the first becomes the shield, the second becomes the cut-out in its middle, then the rest of the middle plays in. Seven middles to compare, each as the mark, an app icon, small sizes and its splash. Loops repeat here; in the app each plays once and stops.
