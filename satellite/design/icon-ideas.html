Satellites in-app icons

Idea board for the icons inside the Satellites app: the bottom bar, the section headers and the list. Three ways to draw each, switched at the top.

Solid is the house and the clipboard's way: one flat shape with the details cut out of it. Solid + Clive's block tucks a small rounded square into a corner and cuts it out where they overlap, the same trick as the app icons, so the icons read as one family. Line draws the same shapes as outlines with round ends. Each card shows the glyph large, at 28 and 20 px (home-screen and header sizes), and in the tinted circle the section headers use, in that section's colour. The small grey name is the Material icon the app uses today.
