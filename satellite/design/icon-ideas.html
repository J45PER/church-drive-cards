Satellites in-app icons

Following the Church Drive icons board: bare glyphs for buttons, a disc with the glyph cut out and a ring for statuses, and one-colour levels. Only what the board doesn't already have.

Already on the board, used as they are. Home (the house) for the list header, and Needs help (the exclamation in its ring) for an error. Nothing to draw. The page and the bottom bar can also use the bare Play, Pause and Stop where a button needs them.

Satellite status. The status icon for the list and the top of a satellite's page, animated as Gregg's are: a disc with the glyph cut out, a ring 11 thick with a gap of 22 that never changes size and only runs, fills, chases or reverses. Idle, Listening, Thinking, Speaking, Updating, Offline, Muted, Weak Wi-Fi.

Levels. Bare line glyphs with round ends, 12 thick, as heavy as the suction levels: Volume in three steps and Brightness in three steps.

Buttons. Bare glyphs for the satellite page's actions. Line, round ends, 12 thick.

The section headers (Design, Temperatures, Glances, Backlight, Sound, Voice, On the satellite, Firmware, Name and room, Presets) stay with the app's own Material icons in their tinted circles, as the main app's headers do, so they are not drawn here. Tell me any you want drawn, and I'll add them in the same way.
