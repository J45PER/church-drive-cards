Black does not save power on this panel

The 3.4" display is an IPS LCD with a backlight, not OLED, so a black picture still lights the whole panel. What saves it is the backlight: dim it, and turn it off after a while with the touch or the wake word bringing it back. The layouts above show how little needs to be lit.

Keep the lit area small and still

Thin numerals, few shapes, no full-width bars. If a layout stays on all night, move it a pixel or two every minute so no edge sits in one place.

Where the mark sits

Low on the glass, about a fifth of the way up from the bottom, so it reads as the thing you talk to and stays out of the way of the clock. It keeps its slow idle drift from the apps (the two squircles sliding together and apart every 4 seconds).
