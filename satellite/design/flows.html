Nothing he says is written on the screen. While he listens, thinks or gives a simple spoken answer the glow is the background and it keeps the clock and glances; it only changes to a card when the answer needs a graphic; then it fades back. Only resting idle is black. Start with talking to him, then the things he does, then quick settings and setting up. Drag down from the top of the screen for quick settings. The companion app (the third app)

One app that adds, names and configures every satellite: the round one, other displays, and the ones with no display. Drawn in the same Clive look: a glow header, white icons, outlined pills and green switches. Two screens: scanning to add one, and setting one up.

Pairing: three kinds of device, one method

A satellite does not need a display to be paired. The one thing every device needs is a way to prove you are standing next to it (a button) and a way to answer (a speaker or a light). The QR code can be on the screen, on a sticker, or both.

Touch display

The round screen, and others

Shows the QR code itself under Settings > Pair or reset, and by itself on first boot. The code is single-use and runs out after 10 minutes. Scan it, the app does the rest.

Small display, no touch

Black and white, 128 × 64

Shows a small QR code and a 6 digit code. One button starts pairing. Scan, or type the six digits if the camera cannot read it.

No display

Speaker or light only

A QR sticker on the underside and the box. Hold the button for 3 seconds: it chimes and the light pulses, and the app asks you to press the button when it chimes again.

How it works for all threeWith
1. A device that has never been set up advertises over Bluetooth and, as a fallback, opens its own Wi-Fi hotspot.ESPHome improv_ble and captive_portal
2. The app scans the QR code (it holds the device’s id and a one-time token, never its permanent key), connects over Bluetooth and sends the home Wi-Fi.The companion app
3. The device joins Wi-Fi. The app gives it a name, a room and a fresh encryption key, and tells Home Assistant to adopt it.The Church Drive integration
4. The device chimes or shows a tick, and the screen comes up on its idle layout.The device

Proposal only. ESPHome has the Bluetooth and hotspot parts and a QR drawing component. The app side, the one-time token and the adoption step are not built or tested.
