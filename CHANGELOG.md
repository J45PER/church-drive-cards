# Changelog

One line per release from the git history (newest first). Detail: HANDOFF.md and docs/HISTORY.md. Android app releases are in companion/android/CHANGELOG.md.

- **v0.106.0 + apps 0.106.0** (2026-11-07): the new colours, phase 1 of the colour overhaul. Every palette except people, the to-do sets and Octopus's electricity and gas is a brighter pastel (the same hues, lighter and a little softer, with a clean neighbouring hue as the accent so the watch and satellite glow blends instead of turning muddy); new names `eco`, `humidity`, `quality_good`, `quality_fair`, `quality_poor` (air quality now goes Good, Fair, Poor in the app), `partly_cloudy`, `cloudy`, `fog`, `wind`, `hail` and `unavailable`; the weather card keeps a neutral ground and its icons take the colour of the condition (partly cloudy is no longer the bright sunny yellow); the humidity graph is the Humidity purple. The climate and alarm panel cards on the dashboards take every colour from the palette names (heating, cooling, eco, humidity, the alarm states) so they follow a changed palette, and a test stops hard-coded colours coming back to them.
- **v0.105.2** (2026-11-07): the Cars, Car charger (Zappi) and Octopus charging plan cards on the dashboards follow the live Car palette (they were fixed to the old teal, so a Car colour change did not show).
- **v0.105.1 + apps 0.105.1** (2026-11-07): the colours set on the review page (17 palettes: alarm Home and Disarmed and Arming Home, Wrong, Needs attention, Blinds, Calendar, Car, Heating, People, Jamie, Diane, Ian, Storm, Sunny, To-do from the house, Shared) are the colours the apps and dashboards start with, as well as the live ones on Home Assistant, so a first launch or an offline phone shows them; the update banner and the phone set-up banner are neutral.
- **v0.105.0 + apps 0.105.0** (2026-11-07): the colours live on Home Assistant. The Church Drive integration keeps the palettes (the shipped ones with your changes on top, and any you add) and serves them (`church_drive/colours`; `church_drive/colours/set` and the `church_drive.set_palette` service change them, administrators only); the phone, To-Do and Satellites apps and the dashboards fetch them and follow `sensor.church_drive_colours`, so a changed colour shows straight away with no app update (the apps keep the last palettes for when the house cannot be reached); `script.clive_show_card` takes the glow palette of each kind of card from the same sensor, so the watch and satellite follow too. Tasks and My to-do are one palette and so are Shopping and Shopping lists (`todo_mine` and `todo_shopping` stay as names for them). `docs/CLIVE-COLOURS.md` says how to change a colour.
- **v0.104.3 + apps 0.104.3** (2026-11-07): text in a palette colour is readable in the dark: a colour too dim for its own dark ground (Gregg, alarm, gas, night, storm, the To-do from the house purple, the people colours) is lifted towards its Light just far enough to reach 4.5:1 (the others are unchanged), which fixes the washed-out pills on the Home to-do card and the Doors & Motion cards; the light row on a zone card (Front light) has its own text colour instead of the zone's purple on orange; a test checks every tone reads.
