Icon morphs, one at a time

Only the icons Clive and the smart speaker can actually show are here (cards, receipts, weather, sport, and the speaker's own screen), not the whole icon set. Every icon morphs out of Clive's two squircles. Pick one (or play them all in turn) and adjust how it arrives: how far the squircles swing, how the body turns, and how the small parts come in. Icons are grouped by base shape so similar ones can be fixed together. Copy the JSON when you are happy and it goes straight into design/clive/cards.json.
