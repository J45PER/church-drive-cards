# Clive's instructions (the live prompt)

A copy of what is in Settings > Devices & services > Claude > Claude conversation > Instructions, as of 2026-10-22 (afternoon) plus the timed heating and fan sentences added on 2026-10-23 (the timed service, and looking after the heating with pause, resume and status), which still have to be pasted into the live prompt. It is a Home Assistant template. Edit it there and re-export it here, or paste this back to restore it.

Lessons built into it, because each one broke something:

- **Only the final message of a turn is spoken.** If he says his answer and then calls a script, Home Assistant speaks only what comes after the script, which was "Anything else?" or nothing at all ("Unable to get response"). So the card and picture scripts are called first, with no words before them, and the whole spoken answer comes last. `clive_show_card` also returns a `next` line telling him to speak.
- **Cards are mandatory** for the weather, scores, fixtures and music. When the paragraph only said he could, a small model never did.
- **He must not assume the person talking is at home.** Asked "who's home" he said "you're home" about someone whose tracker said not home. He now reads every person's state the same way.
- **Digits, not number words.** Written as "seventeen degrees" it showed in the app as words; the prompt asks for "17°C".
- **Do it silently.** He once said "I'll now call the card", which is spoken aloud because it is part of the final message; the prompt now forbids narrating tools.
- **Weather never reaches him through Assist's built-in weather answer**: `weather.forecast_home` is not exposed to Assist, because with `prefer_local_intents` on, Home Assistant answered "What's the weather" itself and he never saw the question. He gets the figures from the card script's result instead.
- **The alarm**: he can arm it (`clive_arm_alarm`), never disarm it, and the sirens and TVs are not exposed.
